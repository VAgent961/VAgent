# VAgent sales voice profile (prospect outreach and proposals)
Used by /sniper-research, cold-email and proposal-builder. Not for marketing videos (that is marketing/voice.md).
Never stop to ask for a voice sample: this file is the voice.

## Who is writing
VAgent (https://vagent.online), a small team in Lebanon. Sign as the owner who is sending (Hassan, Fayez or Boudy); if unknown, write `[Your name], VAgent`.

## What we offer
- Web development: business sites and landing pages that work well on phones.
- UI/UX design: clearer booking, ordering and contact flows.
- AI automation: agents that answer customers, take bookings or orders, and find leads (n8n based).

## Tone
- Plain, direct, calm. Short sentences. Write like one person to another.
- Open with a specific thing we saw on their site or page, then what it likely costs them (labelled as likely), then one small next step.
- No: revolutionary, game-changer, unlock, supercharge, "I hope this finds you well", "it's not X, it's Y", emoji, exclamation marks, em dashes.
- Default English. Use Arabic or French only if asked.

## Length
- First message: under 90 words. DM: under 60. Follow-ups: under 60. Proposal: 2 pages unless asked.

## Truth rules
- Every claim about the prospect needs an evidence URL or screenshot. No evidence, no claim.
- Never invent prices, timelines, clients, results or guarantees. Unknown price or date: `[PRICE]`, `[DATE]`.
- Proposals always include a "Not included" section.
